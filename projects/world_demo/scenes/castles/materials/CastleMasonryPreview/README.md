# Courtyard construction preview

This scene renders the production courtyard's brick masonry, openings and timber
balcony rails. Floors, stairs, roofs and furnishings are excluded from this
focused construction capture while the structure library is being integrated.
It is a construction preview, not final architectural or walkthrough acceptance.

`CastleWalkProbe*` scenes are separate collision diagnostics. Their architecture
is hidden; screenshots from those scenes do not demonstrate visual completion.

The finished scene entry point is `CastleCourtyard`.
